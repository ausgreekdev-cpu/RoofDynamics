RoofDynamics Field is the on-site companion for trade-qualified roofing and plumbing crews in Perth, Western Australia.

Built for the roof, not the office:
- Digital SWMS with one-tap sign-off for work above 2.0 m
- Photo capture for before/after roof condition and hazard evidence
- Geo-tagged leads and quotes with GPS
- Offline-first: every form works without signal and syncs on reconnect
- Live stock levels across yard, ute and truck
- Aerial measurement with pitch, waste and sheet cut-length math
- WA compliance: Security of Payment 15-business-day countdown, BA1/BA2 permits, MyLeave WA

RoofDynamics Field is part of the RoofDynamics platform by AusGreek Developments. Sign in with your RoofDynamics account to sync data. Demo mode works offline with no account.

Privacy: photos and location are only captured when you explicitly use those features, and are stored against your job records. See the privacy policy for details.
