# Privacy Policy — RoofDynamics Field

Last updated: 2026-11-19. Contact: ausgreekdev@gmail.com

## Data collected
- **Account data:** email, name, tenant/company name, role. Used to authenticate and scope data to your organisation.
- **Job & quote data:** project details, customer contact details, measurements, invoices, compliance records. Created by you or your crew.
- **Photos:** roof/condition/hazard photos captured only when you use the camera feature. Stored against the related job.
- **Location:** GPS coordinates captured only when you use geo-tagging (leads/quotes). Not tracked in the background.
- **Device/usage:** standard crash and performance telemetry (Sentry) when enabled.

## How data is used
To provide the RoofDynamics Field service: sync your jobs, quotes, inventory and compliance records across devices, generate reports, and support your crew on site.

## Storage & retention
Data is stored in your organisation's tenant scope. Demo/sandbox data is stored locally on your device (localStorage/IndexedDB) and never leaves it. Production data is stored on the RoofDynamics backend (PostgreSQL) and object storage (R2).

## Sharing
We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the app (hosting, error reporting) under contract.

## Your rights
You can request export or deletion of your organisation's data by contacting us. You can revoke camera/location permissions at any time in your device settings; the app will continue to work without them (manual entry).

## Children
RoofDynamics Field is a business productivity tool and is not directed at children.
